How to get your app credentials

  1. Open the LinkedIn Developer Portal and choose Create app. Complete the required details and associate a LinkedIn Page; LinkedIn may ask you to verify it.
  2. In Products, enable Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect and Share on LinkedIn. Wait until both are approved.
  3. Open Auth, copy the Client ID and Client Secret, and add the redirect URL shown here under Authorized redirect URLs.
  4. Paste the credentials here, save them, then choose Connect LinkedIn and approve access on LinkedIn.

Why are these needed? The client ID identifies your app; the secret proves its identity when it exchanges your LinkedIn approval for an access token. That token lets the app publish for you. You sign in on LinkedIn, so this app never asks for your LinkedIn password. Keep the client secret private.

Authorized redirect URL
http://localhost:8000/auth/linkedin/callback
